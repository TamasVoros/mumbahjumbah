# Bingo Sites Research: Summary

Prepared 1 Oct 2026. Context: exploring a "reverse" / meeting-buzzword bingo game.

**How to read this document.** Every claim is tagged:
- **[Sourced]**: stated by a source I found (link given). I did not independently verify the source's accuracy.
- **[Estimate]**: third-party or secondary estimate, not official.
- **[Speculation]**: my own assumption or inference. Not evidence.
- **[Not found]**: I searched and could not find it.

Overall limits: this is a quick desk search, not an audit. Private companies' revenue is generally not public. No tax filings, company registry records or audited accounts were found for any small site mentioned here.

---

## 1. Landscape and categories

| Category | What it is | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Buzzword / meeting bingo generators | Single-purpose pages that produce random cards of corporate jargon | MiniWebtool and OnlineMiniTools generators, buzzwordbingogame.com, Meeting Bingo (useless-toolkit.com) | **[Sourced]** One generator offers 100+ buzzwords in 5 categories and 3x3 / 4x4 / 5x5 grids; auto-detects a win. buzzwordbingogame.com says it has run since 1999. |
| B. General card makers | Build any themed card, print or play via link | Bingo Baker, Bingo Card Creator, myfreebingocards, Print-Bingo | **[Sourced]** Bingo Baker: share a link and each player gets their own card; large library of ready-made cards. Bingo Card Creator advertises up to 2,000 online players. |
| C. Hosted event / live platforms | Organiser runs a game for a crowd, with leaderboards and branding | Crowdpurr, Bingo Maker (bingomaker.com) | **[Sourced]** Crowdpurr: live rankings, quiz and team modes, custom logos, lead capture, live/virtual/hybrid. Bingo Maker: Webex app for up to 1,000 players; free trial of one hour / 25 attendees. |
| D. Callers / utilities | Number callers, win checkers | Let's Play Bingo | **[Sourced]** Free, ad-free, 1,000+ patterns, offline use. |
| E. Gambling and social-casino bingo | Real-money or in-app-purchase games | Bingo Blitz (Playtika), Buzz Bingo, Mecca, Jackpotjoy | A different industry from A-D. |

Features that differ between sites **[Sourced / general pattern]**: print-only vs live multiplayer, player caps, automatic win detection, branding, theme libraries, AI card generation (Crowdpurr announced one in its Summer 2026 updates), and Zoom/Teams/Webex integration.

**[Speculation]** I did not find a product built around the "reverse" mechanic (marking off what is actually said in a live meeting, on a shared board). That could mean a gap or could mean no demand. I haven't tested either.

## 2. Business models

| Model | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Free with ads | **[Sourced, historical]** Bingo.com's 2005 results: about $1.98M total revenue, of which about $1.39M was advertising and about $0.59M was cash bingo. Later it shifted toward its own real-money games. |
| Freemium upgrade | **[Sourced]** An industry write-up lists typical paywalled items: player caps, watermarks, guaranteed-unique cards, live multiplayer. **[Sourced, dated]** A 2023 blog says Bingo Baker premium was $24.95 lifetime; I did not confirm the current price. |
| Tiered subscription by audience size | **[Sourced, dated]** Crowdpurr: free plan (20 participants, shows Crowdpurr branding and sponsor ads); paid from $49.99/month. A G2 answer from about 3 years ago listed $49.99 (100 participants), $149.99 (500), $249.99 (1,000), $499.99 (5,000). Current prices not re-verified. |
| Trial to paid | **[Sourced]** Bingo Maker free trial: one 1-hour session, up to 25 attendees. |
| Niche software sold to a specific group | **[Sourced, self-reported]** Bingo Card Creator: current owner bought it in 2015; says about $1.5K/month; about 60% of customers are teachers; about 55% of traffic organic and about 30% from pre-made card pages. |
| In-app purchases / subscriptions | **[Sourced]** Mobile bingo apps list weekly/monthly/yearly VIP subscriptions and booster packs on app-store pages. |
| Real-money play | **[Sourced]** Buzz Bingo, Mecca etc. (see section 3). |

## 5. Scenario sketch (my own arithmetic, not research)

Assumptions: **[Speculation]** $150/month entry price for a hypothetical product of your own; revenue is gross.

| | 10 customers | 100 customers |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | $1,500 | $15,000 |
| Full year (all from day one) | $18,000 | $180,000 |
| Year one if signups spread evenly | about $9,000 | about $90,000 |

- **[Speculation]** Costs: payment fees of about 3%, and hosting/tools of about $100/month (10 customers) to $300/month (100 customers), are my guesses. Taxes, VAT and refunds are not modelled.
- **[Speculation]** The check that $220K / ($150 x 12) is about 122 customers is simple arithmetic against an unverified revenue estimate. It does not prove Crowdpurr's actual customer count or price mix.
- **Your premise, not verified:** that the platform needs little maintenance. I found no evidence either way. Customer acquisition, support, integration changes (Zoom/Teams/Webex) and churn are real costs that this sketch ignores.
